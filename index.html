A painter’s pocket guide to colour

Colour Companion shows you how to shade a colour, which paints to mix to get it, and what goes with it. For iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

Shade

Pick a colour and see its shadow, its light, a background for it and the colours that go with it.

Mix

Tick the tubes you own. The app tells you which to mix, in simple parts, for any colour.

Photo

Choose a picture and get its main colours as a palette, each with a recipe.

Ideas

Ready-made mood palettes, sets for metals, neons, skin and skies, and colour theory to play with.

Studio

Keep colours in groups, share them as a picture, and export to Procreate, Photoshop and more.

Private by design

There is no account, no adverts and no tracking. Your colours and photos stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Free and Pro

Shade and colour theory are free. Colour Companion Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks recipes, photo palettes, mood palettes, special sets, saving and export. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
