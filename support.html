Subscription

I paid for Pro but it’s locked

Open Studio → Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel?

Open Studio → Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also go to the iPhone’s Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your colours

How do I move my colours to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Studio → Settings → Back up and save the file, for example to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, open Studio → Settings → Restore… and choose that file.

How do I move a colour to another group?

In Studio, press and hold the colour, then drag it onto the other group. Or press and hold and choose Move to.

How do I get a palette into Procreate?

In Studio, tap the ⋯ button on a group, choose Export, then Procreate. In the share sheet, choose Procreate. The palette appears in Procreate’s Palettes list. Procreate palettes hold up to 30 colours.

Recipes

What does the orange “!” on a colour mean?

The paints you’ve ticked can’t mix that colour closely. Tap it to see the nearest mix and which paint would help. You can change your paints in Studio → Paints.

My mix doesn’t match the screen exactly

Recipes are a starting point. Paint brands differ, and screens glow in a way paint can’t. Mix a little, compare, and adjust.

Why do metals and neons have no recipe?

Those sets are made for digital painting. Paint can only get close to them, so tapping one copies its colour code.
