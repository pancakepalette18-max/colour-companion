Privacy policy

Your colours stay with you

Colour Companion does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Colour Companion is made by Pancake Palette Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. You can reach us at pancakepalette18@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves these things on your device only:

  • the colours and groups you save;
  • which paints you say you own;
  • your settings, and your first name if you choose to type one in.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server and cannot see it. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. You can also erase it at any time in Studio → Settings → Erase everything.

Photos

When you choose a photo, the app reads its colours on your device. The photo is not uploaded, not stored by the app and not shared. The app only sees the photos you pick, not your whole library.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, card details or Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active, and gives us sales totals that do not identify you. Apple’s handling of your data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Things you choose to share

If you export a palette, share a palette picture or make a backup file, the file goes only where you send it, for example to another app, to Files or to a message. What happens next is up to that app or service.

Analytics, adverts and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics tools, no advertising and no third-party tracking code.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and help you, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The company that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data held about you, including the right to see it, correct it or have it deleted. Because the app collects no personal data, the only data we may hold is an email you have sent us. To use your rights, email us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
